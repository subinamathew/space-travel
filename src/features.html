Accessibility Standards (Dec 2026)

RocketRun is engineered from the ground up to ensure every curious mind, classroom explorer, and astronomer can experience our solar system without barrier.

⌨️

Universal Keyboard Navigation

Complete focus management and keyboard shortcuts: Space or p pauses orbital motion, 1–9 triggers instant interplanetary transit, ArrowLeft/ArrowRight navigates adjacent worlds, and Esc dismisses all dialogs.

👁️

Visual Ergonomics & Night Dimming

High-contrast text exceeding 7:1 against cosmic voids. Automatic mobile/tablet solar dimming (18:00–07:00) scales the Sun between 5% and 20% dimmer to reduce circadian retinal fatigue during evening exploration.

🦻

Polite Screen Reader Telemetry

Assistive speech and braille technologies receive live telemetry broadcasts via aria-live="polite" regions. Orbital velocity, mass metrics, and Earth clock updates are conveyed without speech synthesizer buffer clogging.

👆

Motor Accessibility (≥ 44×44px Targets)

All interactive switches, buttons, and close controls strictly observe minimum 44×44px hit-box bounding boxes to support young children, motor-impaired users, and touch-screen devices.

🌀

Vestibular & Reduced Motion

Strictly obeys @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce). Automatically neutralizes high-speed orbital spins, warp preloader jumps, and Star Wars crawl tilts, replacing them with static, calm views.

🌐

9-Language Native Typography

Full translation key parity across English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Japanese, Chinese, Malayalam, and Tamil, honoring regional script rendering and typographic rhythm.

ASTROPHYSICAL ATLAS Curated from NASA & JPL

Planetary Science & Lore

Beyond standard school metrics, delve into the extraordinary physical curiosities of our celestial neighborhood.

☀️

The Sun (Sol)

The Coronal Heating Mystery

The visible surface of the Sun (photosphere) sits at ~5,500°C, yet the outer corona inexplicably surges to 1,000,000°C to 3,000,000°C! Scientists hypothesize magnetic nanoflares and Alfvén waves constantly pump magnetic energy into the solar atmosphere.

☿️

Mercury

3:2 Spin-Orbit Resonance & Polar Ice

Mercury spins exactly three times on its axis for every two orbits around the Sun. Because of this lock, a single solar day (sunrise to sunrise) lasts 176 Earth days! Permanently shadowed craters at its poles harbor billions of tons of water ice shielded from blistering 430°C sunlight.

♀️

Venus

Runaway Greenhouse & Backward Spin

With an atmosphere 96.5% carbon dioxide generating 92 bars of pressure (equal to being 900 meters under Earth’s ocean), Venus suffers a runaway greenhouse effect reaching 465°C. Venus also spins clockwise in retrograde—the Sun rises in the west!

🌍

Earth

The Protective Geodynamo

Earth's molten iron outer core generates a powerful magnetosphere that deflects dangerous ionizing solar wind. Combined with active plate tectonics that balance atmospheric carbon, Earth maintains the only known stable surface oceans in the cosmos.

♂️

Mars

Colossal Geology: Olympus Mons

Mars hosts Olympus Mons, a shield volcano that reaches 21.9 km above datum—nearly three times the height of Mt. Everest! Its canyon system, Valles Marineris, is 4,000 km long, 200 km wide, and up to 7 km deep, dwarfing the Grand Canyon.

♃

Jupiter

Metallic Hydrogen & Anticyclones

Deep inside Jupiter, pressures exceed 2 million atmospheres, forcing hydrogen into a shimmering liquid metal ocean that conducts electricity and powers a magnetosphere 20,000 times stronger than Earth’s. The Great Red Spot is an anticyclone larger than Earth itself.

🪐

Saturn

Floating Density & Hexagonal Jet Stream

Saturn's average density is 0.687 g/cm³—less dense than water! If you found a bathtub large enough, Saturn would float. Its majestic rings span 282,000 km yet average just 10 meters thick, while a bizarre geometric hexagon storm rotates at its north pole.

♅

Uranus

The Sideways Rolling World

Uranus has an axial tilt of 97.77°, likely caused by a massive protoplanetary collision billions of years ago. It rolls around the Sun like a ball, plunging each pole into 42 years of continuous darkness followed by 42 years of unsetting sunlight.

♆

Neptune

Supersonic Winds & Cryovolcanic Triton

Neptune is the windiest planet, where methane storms whip through the atmosphere at supersonic speeds exceeding 2,100 km/h (1,300 mph). Its largest moon, Triton, was captured from the Kuiper Belt and spews plumes of liquid nitrogen from active cryogeysers.
